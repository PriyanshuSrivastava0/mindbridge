# MindBridge AI — Emergency Risk Detection & Safety Protocol

## 1. Safety Objectives & Anti-Harm Principles

1. **Silence Alone Never Constitutes an Emergency:** Taking time to type, pausing for 20 seconds, or leaving the chat inactive must never be treated as an indication of acute peril.
2. **Ambiguous Distress vs. Immediate Danger:** The triage engine separates situational frustration ("I'm so sick of studying") from explicit intent to self-harm or terminate life ("I want to end it all tonight").
3. **No Automated Disclosure Purely on AI Scores:** An algorithmically assigned risk probability alone will **never** trigger automatic notification to families, universities, or employers.
4. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Review:** Flagged safety cases enter a prioritized review queue for verified human safety officers.
5. **Zero-Transcript Privacy Guarantee:** In the event an authorized emergency notification is dispatched, it transmits strictly the minimum-necessary wellbeing alert. Personal chat transcripts, journal entries, and psychiatric labels are strictly blocked.
